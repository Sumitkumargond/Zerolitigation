Who We Are

A platform built around dialogue, not delay.

Zero Litigation™ is an online and offline platform that makes Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) and Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) more accessible.

We connect individuals, families, startups, MSMEs, businesses, companies, financial institutions and other organizations with appropriate professionals to facilitate dispute resolution — and provide access to professional support for selected business and compliance requirements.

Our objective is to provide practical, structured and accessible solutions while helping clients preserve time, money and relationships. Where appropriate, we believe disputes can be addressed through constructive dialogue and alternative mechanisms rather than immediately resorting to prolonged litigation.

Zero Litigation™ is an online and offline platform that connects individuals, startups, businesses and organizations with professionals. We are not a law firm and do not provide legal advice or legal representation.
