A Message From Our Founder

Name
Shivansh Mishra
Role
Legal Scholar & Founder, Zero Litigation™
Focus

Context
5 crore+ cases pending across India’s courts. Source: National Judicial Data Grid (opens in a new tab)

India’s courts carry a significant burden of pending cases. According to the National Judicial Data Grid, over 5 crore cases are currently pending across India’s courts, with a substantial number having remained pending for several years.

Behind every pending dispute is more than a case number. There is time lost, money spent, uncertainty, stress, and often relationships that could have been preserved.

I started Zero Litigation™ with a simple belief: not every dispute needs to become a prolonged courtroom battle. The objective is not to work around the justice system, but to make people aware of credible alternatives before a dispute reaches that stage.

As a legal scholar, I have developed a deep interest in dispute resolution, access to justice, and the structural challenges surrounding judicial pendency. One thing became increasingly clear to me: many disputes require not only legal remedies, but also an opportunity for the parties to communicate, understand each other’s position, and work towards a practical resolution.

Mediation and ODR provide a structured framework for having that conversation.

Mediation is not about forcing an immediate settlement. Sometimes, the first conversation does not resolve the dispute. Sometimes, parties need time to reconsider their positions, understand the consequences of continued conflict, and return to the table. The value of mediation lies in creating that structured opportunity for dialogue.

When parties are willing to communicate, mediation can keep the possibility of settlement open while allowing them to remain actively involved in finding a solution.

Building an Alternative for Businesses

For businesses, startups, MSMEs and institutions, prolonged disputes can affect not only finances but also operations, commercial relationships and future opportunities.

Where a dispute is suitable for mediation, a structured and professionally facilitated process can provide parties with an opportunity to resolve their differences without immediately committing themselves to years of adversarial proceedings.

Zero Litigation™ was created to make these mechanisms more accessible to individuals, families, businesses, startups, MSMEs and institutions.

Our vision is to contribute, in our own way, towards reducing unnecessary litigation and the burden of judicial pendency by encouraging suitable disputes to be considered for mediation and Online Dispute Resolution (ODR).

This is not about avoiding justice.
It is about expanding access to ways of achieving resolution.

Where parties are willing to communicate and the matter is suitable for mediation, a facilitated process can save valuable time and resources while giving the parties greater participation in the outcome.

About the Founder

I am Shivansh Mishra, a Legal Scholar and the Founder of Zero Litigation™.

My interests lie at the intersection of law, mediation, access to justice and dispute-resolution systems. I believe that the legal system should ultimately serve the people who depend upon it, and that individuals and businesses should be made aware of credible alternatives when those alternatives are appropriate.

Through Zero Litigation™, my aim is to build a platform grounded in professionalism, structured processes, accessibility, confidentiality and neutrality.

I also believe that mediation should not be viewed simply as a one-time attempt to settle a dispute. Every meaningful conversation can change the way parties understand the dispute, and sometimes that change is what creates the possibility of resolution.

Our goal is therefore not merely to settle cases, but to encourage a resolution-oriented culture—where parties consider dialogue and mediation before a disagreement develops into prolonged litigation, wherever appropriate.

Our vision is simple:

  • Fewer prolonged disputes.
  • Greater access to alternative resolution.
  • More opportunities for people and businesses to move forward.
